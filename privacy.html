Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

CodeRapid Poster ("the app") is a private tool the CodeRapid channel owner uses to upload CodeRapid's own videos to the channel's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the connected TikTok account (display name and account ID), to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos to that account.

Where it is stored

Access tokens are stored only in a configuration file on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server other than the platform they belong to (TikTok or Google), and they are never shared with or sold to anyone.

What the app does not do

The app does not collect data about viewers or any other users, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not read messages, followers or other account data.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions, after which the stored token stops working.
